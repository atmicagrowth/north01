# Photography candidates, colour — a second hundred

The companion to [`PHOTOGRAPHY_CANDIDATES.md`](PHOTOGRAPHY_CANDIDATES.md), which is deliberately
monochrome. This set answers a different brief, in the owner's words: *"these pictures need to have
more colour, they will be the things with colour in the pages."*

That is the right instinct for this design. `NORTH01_Visual_Guide_OnlineOnly.md` sets a palette of
obsidian, charcoal, bone and stone and says *"avoid highly saturated or overly artificial grading"* —
a rule about the **interface**, not about what the photographs inside it may contain. A page built from
black and bone with one saturated image in it reads as expensive; the same colour spread across the
furniture would not.

## What "colour" means here

Deep colour, not bright colour. The ranking that produced the first hundred was inverted — most
saturated first — and then everything neon-bright was thrown out by eye: no pink cosmos, no powder
pigments, no primary-coloured flower macros, nothing with the look of a screensaver. What survived is
colour that sits on a dark ground: **indigo, oxblood, ochre, rust, moss, tobacco, terracotta, glacier
blue, and neon on black**.

100 images, none of them in the first hundred. Same rules as that list: **CC0 or CC BY only**, NC and
ND excluded, minimum 1,807 px, **median 4,608 px**.

## Using them

- **One per surface, not one per page.** These are the accent. If two land on the same screen the
  effect is gone.
- **The cloth group is the safest bet** for category tiles and texture panels: it is colour that is
  literally fabric, which is what the shop sells.
- **The night group is the safest bet for a hero**, because the frame is 80% black already and the
  type has somewhere to live.
- Same import route as before: download the full file into `brand-media`, add a line to the placement
  map in `scripts/import-brand-media.ts`, run `pnpm import:media`.

- **Licence:** 99 CC0 or public domain, 1 CC BY 4.0 (credit line below).
  **rawpixel needs a free account**; StockSnap, Wikimedia and WordPress Photos do not.
- **People:** four frames contain a person, all of them distant, from behind or under an umbrella. No
  identifiable faces, for the reason the first list gives — a CC licence is never a model release.

## What this still does not solve

- **Your garments.** One red skirt and two pairs of shoes are not a product shoot (`TODO.md` §5).
- **A palette.** A hundred photographers means a hundred versions of "red". Pick three or four colours
  and take only the frames that agree with them, or the shop will look like a mood board.
